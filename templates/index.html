          How CanLand Works

          1. Enter Property

          Provide an address, legal description, or parcel details anywhere in Canada.

          2. Auto-Match

          We geocode the address and match it to the correct municipality and provincial zoning system.

          3. Zoning Analysis

          Retrieve zoning classification, permitted uses, setbacks, density limits, and bylaw references.

          4. Download Report

          Export a professional PDF feasibility report ready for planners, investors, and consultants.
